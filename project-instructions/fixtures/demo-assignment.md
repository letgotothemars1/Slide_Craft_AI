# Synthetic course demo assignment

Prepare a five-slide presentation for a master's seminar on digital systems in public services. Your audience is course examiners and classmates who have not worked on waste sorting technology. Use the fictional North Campus sorting pilot in the attached source PDF as a case study. The presentation should take no more than four minutes.

Explain the sorting problem, the proposed camera-assisted workflow, the pilot observations, the operating trade-offs, and a measured conclusion. Include at least one page-numbered reference to the source PDF when discussing the pilot observations. Distinguish observations from causal claims: the synthetic pilot has no randomized control group and cannot prove that the system caused the change. Do not present the figures as real research or a real campus result.

Keep the five-slide order easy to follow and use concise English. The presentation must preserve the exact qualification “promising, not proven” in the conclusion. Do not add external statistics, invented citations, or recommendations about deploying the system beyond this fictional campus. The final PPTX should remain editable so the student can correct wording before presenting.
