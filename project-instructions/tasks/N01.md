# N01 — Safe academic demo material

**Owner:** non-coding classmate. **Difficulty:** 1/5. **Budget:** half a day. **Start:** week 1. **Status:** ready to start.

**Outcome:** developers receive one realistic master's-level assignment and one source PDF that can be used in a public course demo.

**Do:** choose a narrow topic for a five-slide report or project presentation. Write a 150–250 word assignment brief with audience, goal, time limit, three required points, and one instruction the AI must not change. Provide one short public, synthetic, or team-authored PDF with at least two pages and record its bibliographic details and source/permission. Prepare a sample Context Pack in English with thesis, three claims, terms, constraints, and one unresolved question. Mark any invented facts as synthetic. Never include private student data or copyrighted course handouts without permission.

**Deliverables:** put `demo-assignment.md`, `demo-context-pack.md`, and the PDF under `project-instructions/fixtures/` and give the developers the exact claim/page pairs they can use for citation checks. The PDF may be created using a normal document editor; no coding is needed.

**Done when:** a new person can understand the topic, the PDF has at least one fact on each of two pages, and the team can build five coherent slides from the material.
